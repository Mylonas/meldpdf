# Highest Interest Rates on Savings Accounts in Cyprus: A Complete Audit (2026)

*Last updated: October 2026*

Cyprus savers have long struggled with some of the lowest deposit rates in the eurozone. As of mid-2026, the Central Bank of Cyprus reported an average rate of just **1.35%** on new household deposits with up to one-year maturity, compared with approximately **2.03%** across the eurozone. But where exactly can you find the best returns? This article audits every major bank and electronic money institution (EMI) operating in Cyprus to find the highest savings rates, whether fixed-term or flexible.

---

## Key Findings at a Glance

- **Highest fixed deposit rate available to Cyprus residents:** 3.08% (Lidion Bank, Malta, via PickTheBank, 2-5 year terms)
- **Highest domestic Cyprus bank fixed deposit rate:** ~1.66% average (Ancoria Bank, up to 1 year)
- **Highest instant-access savings rate:** ~2.25-3.00% (Trade Republic, Germany), ~2.50-2.75% (bunq, Netherlands)
- **No domestic Cypriot bank** offers a competitive instant-access savings rate -- most pay 0.00% to 0.05%
- **RCB Bank** no longer operates as a bank (licence surrendered in 2022)

## Part 5: Deposit Guarantee Coverage

Understanding which scheme protects your money is essential.

### Cyprus Deposit Guarantee Scheme (DGS)
- **Coverage:** EUR 100,000 per depositor per institution
- **Covers:** All deposits (EUR and foreign currency) at licensed Cyprus banks
- **Protected institutions:** Bank of Cyprus, Eurobank Cyprus, Alpha Bank Cyprus, Ancoria Bank, AstroBank, NBG Cyprus, CDB, Housing Finance Corporation

### Foreign EU Deposit Guarantee Schemes
- **Lidion Bank:** Maltese DGS (EUR 100,000)
- **Revolut Instant Access Savings:** Lithuanian DGS (EUR 100,000)
- **Trade Republic:** German DGS (EUR 100,000 per partner bank)
- **bunq:** Dutch DGS (EUR 100,000)

### NOT Deposit-Insured
- **Revolut Flexible Cash Funds:** Investment product, no DGS coverage
- **Wise:** Money market fund, no DGS coverage
- **EMI balances generally:** Safeguarded but not deposit-insured

## Part 6: Tax Considerations

Interest earned by Cyprus tax residents is subject to:

| Tax | Rate | Applies To |
|-----|------|------------|
| Special Defence Contribution (SDC) | 17% | Domiciled Cyprus tax residents |
| General Healthcare System (GHS) | 2.65% | All Cyprus tax residents |
| **Effective total** | **~19.65%** | **Domiciled residents** |

**Non-domiciled Cyprus tax residents** are exempt from SDC for 17 years from becoming tax resident. They pay only the 2.65% GHS contribution on interest income.

Banks may withhold tax at source. Always verify your specific situation with a tax advisor.

## Conclusion

The gap between what Cypriot domestic banks pay and what is available through cross-border EU options is striking. While Bank of Cyprus and Eurobank pay 0.00% on savings, institutions like Lidion Bank (via PickTheBank) offer over 3% on fixed deposits, and fintechs like Trade Republic and bunq offer 2.25-2.75% on instant-access balances.

For savers willing to use cross-border platforms, the rewards are significant. For those preferring to bank domestically, Ancoria Bank stands out as the clear leader among Cypriot banks, though its rates still lag far behind eurozone averages.

**All rates should be verified directly with each institution before making any decisions, as they change frequently.**
